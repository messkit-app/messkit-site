What it does

Most kitchen apps ask you to type in your food. Messkit is built around getting the contents of a real kitchen in quickly, and keeping it honest afterwards.

  • Add things by scanning them — a barcode, a label, a whole shelf, or a receipt.
  • Pooled quantities. Two jars of the same thing are one amount, and an open jar counts for what is left in it.
  • Dates that reflect where food is kept. Moving something to the freezer changes how long it keeps, and the app suggests the new date rather than deciding for you.
  • Cooking takes stock out. What you cook comes off what you have.
  • Built for more than one country. English, Arabic, French and Spanish, right-to-left included, with metric and imperial.
